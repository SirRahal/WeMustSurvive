**Game Design Document – Working Title: Belt & Barricade**  
*(Post-Apocalyptic Factory Defense – 2-Player Co-op + Solo)*

---

### 1. High Concept

A 2-player cooperative (and solo) factory-defense game set on a post-apocalyptic Earth under alien invasion. Players control mobile engineer avatars who must gather resources, build automated production lines, and construct layered defenses to protect a central Nexus from increasingly intense waves of aliens.

The game blends Factorio-style logistics and progression with wave-based tower defense. Every wall, turret, and piece of ammunition is produced by the factory the players themselves design and maintain.

**Target session length**  
- Training map: ~25 minutes  
- Standard maps: 45–60 minutes

---

### 2. Core Gameplay Loop

1. Mine resources using drills.
2. Transport materials with belts and inserters.
3. Refine and process resources.
4. Research new technologies.
5. Build and upgrade walls, turrets, and power infrastructure.
6. Defend the Nexus against timed alien waves.
7. Expand outward to claim richer resource zones.
8. Eventually construct late-game super weapon buildings.

Between waves, players have a preparation window to expand, repair, and re-optimize their factory.

---

### 3. Player Characters

Players control mobile **Engineer** avatars.

**Capabilities**
- Move freely around the map.
- Build any structure (as long as the required resources exist in the shared network).
- Repair buildings and walls.
- Carry a basic personal weapon for self-defense.
- Issue orders that show a visible path line and highlight the target structure for the other player.

Both players have identical capabilities and full access to the shared factory. There are no rigid roles.

---

### 4. Resources & Progression

Five resources form a clear tier ladder:

| Tier | Resource | Primary Role |
|------|----------|--------------|
| 0    | Coal     | Early power generation |
| 1    | Iron     | Basic structures, belts, walls, gun turrets |
| 2    | Copper   | Power grid, electronics, laser turrets, better logistics |
| 3    | Silver   | Advanced mining, heavy defenses, mid-tier research |
| 4    | Gold     | End-game turrets, super weapons, highest-tier efficiency |

- Richer and deeper veins appear farther from the starting area.
- **Claim Zones** are pre-marked on the map. Simply building a drill inside a claim zone grants higher yield and faster mining speed. Aliens can still destroy these mining facilities.

---

### 5. Buildings & Tiered Defenses

**Walls & Towers scale with resource tiers**

| Resource | Wall Tier       | Tower Examples              |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------------------|
| Iron     | Basic Wall      | Gun Turret                  |
| Copper   | Reinforced Wall | Laser Turret                |
| Silver   | Heavy Wall      | Rocket Turret / Flame Turret|
| Gold     | Fortress Wall   | Heavy / Advanced Turret     |

**Key Building Categories**
- Mining Drills (tiered)
- Belts, Underground Belts, Splitters, Inserters (multiple speed tiers)
- Power generators and power poles
- Refineries / Furnaces
- Laboratories
- Storage (Nexus acts as primary storage)
- Walls and Turrets
- Super Weapon buildings (Nuclear Silo, Satellite Uplink, etc.)

Players can place buildings as long as the required materials exist anywhere in the shared logistics network.

---

### 6. Power System

Full Factorio-style power grid.  
Players must generate power and distribute it via power poles. Insufficient power causes brownouts that disable production buildings and turrets.

---

### 7. Research

Research is fully shared.  
Each technology requires a mix of resources from current and previous tiers. Higher technologies demand Silver and eventually Gold, forcing territorial expansion.

---

### 8. Combat & Waves

**Wave System**
- Waves arrive on a strict timer.
- A short preparation phase exists between waves (players may end it early).
- Enemies target the Nexus as their primary objective but will attack resource nodes, belts, drills, power poles, and turrets when convenient.

**Enemy Types (Map 1 Roster)**
- **Grunt** – Basic swarm unit
- **Runner** – Fast, low health
- **Brute** – Slow, high health tank
- **Spitter** – Ranged acid attacker (targets structures)
- **Sapper** – Prioritizes logistics and power infrastructure

**Loss Condition**  
The Nexus is destroyed.

**Nexus**  
Serves as the main storage, provides starting power, and has a small amount of passive defense.

---

### 9. Map 1 – “The Funnel” (Training Map)

**Layout**  
V-shaped map. Nexus sits at the bottom of the V. Unbuildable terrain forms the arms, forcing all enemies through a single choke point at the top.

**Resource Placement**
- Coal and Iron near the Nexus
- Copper halfway up the arms
- One Silver claim zone just behind the main choke

**Duration**  
Approximately 25 minutes across 10 waves.

**Progression Teaching Goals**
- Waves 1–4: Iron-tier mining, belts, basic walls, and gun turrets
- Waves 5–6: Copper, power grid, and laser turrets
- Waves 7–10: Claiming the Silver zone and mixing higher-tier defenses

---

### 10. Super Weapons

Late-game strategic weapons that require dedicated buildings:

- **Nuclear Silo** → Enables Nuke
- **Satellite Uplink / Launch Tower** → Enables Orbital Strike

These buildings are expensive (heavy Silver + Gold) and produce powerful but high-cooldown abilities that any player can activate once constructed.

---

### 11. Meta Progression (Between Maps)

Completing maps awards **Meta Points** spent on a large Path of Exile 2-style skill tree.

**Major Clusters**
- Extraction
- Logistics & Belts
- Power & Efficiency
- Defense
- Research Speed
- Super Weapons

Nodes are deliberately interleaved so players cannot pure-focus a single area. Progressing deep into Defense requires taking some Logistics or Power nodes, and vice versa. Most bonuses are small incremental improvements (+4% to +8%), with stronger Notable nodes inside clusters.

---

### 12. Solo Mode

- Enemy health, damage, and spawn counts are reduced so threats are approximately **55% weaker** than in co-op.
- All other systems (timers, building costs, research) remain identical.
- Designed so a single skilled player experiences similar tension to a co-op pair.

---

### 13. Co-op Communication

- **Alt + Command Wheel** (League of Legends style) for quick pings (“Defend”, “Need Copper”, “Danger”, “On my way”, etc.).
- **Freehand drawing** on the map (Beyond All Reason style) for more detailed communication.
- When an engineer receives a move/build/repair order, a clear path line appears on the ground and the target structure is highlighted for both players.

---

### 14. Theme & Visual Style

**Setting**: Post-apocalyptic Earth under alien invasion.  
**Art Direction**: Clean, modern, readable Factorio-like aesthetic. Clear silhouettes, strong contrast between player structures and alien threats, and easily distinguishable resource types and belt contents.

---

### 15. Design Pillars (Summary)

1. **Factory is the weapon** – Everything used in defense is produced by the players’ logistics network.
2. **Meaningful expansion** – Stronger resources and claim zones sit behind increasing risk.
3. **Clear progression** – Each resource tier visibly upgrades both production and defensive capabilities.
4. **Co-op clarity** – Strong communication tools and shared ownership keep both players equally engaged.
5. **Readable tension** – Wave timers and the V-shaped funnel create understandable, fair pressure.
